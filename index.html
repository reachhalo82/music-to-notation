Upload a recording of one melodic line — humming, whistling, singing, or a single instrument — and get it back as notation with a tempo, key signature and rests. Full mixes and chords won't separate cleanly.

Rhythm is snapped to an eighth-note grid at the detected tempo, in 4/4 from the first note. Repeated notes with no pause between them may merge into one. Only the first 90 seconds are analysed.
